Study overview

This replication study applies latent class analysis to the MIMIC-III dataset to identify distinct multimorbidity subgroups among ICU patients.

The study found differences in sepsis risk and clinical severity across these subgroups, highlighting their potential value for improving risk stratification in critical care.
